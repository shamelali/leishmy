# Malaysia's MUA "DM-Chase" Is Over: Leish! Launches Real-Time Beauty Booking Platform

### New marketplace lets brides, event-goers and studios book vetted makeup artists with live availability, instant confirmation and secure payment — no more DM-ing 15 artists and waiting days for a quote.

**KUALA LUMPUR, 5 October 2026** — Leish! (leish.my), Malaysia's new beauty booking marketplace, officially launches today with a simple promise: *never DM for a date again.* The platform connects clients across Kuala Lumpur & Selangor with vetted makeup artists and studios — covering Bridal, Event, Hijab, Editorial, Airbrush, SFX, Hair and Lash — and lets them book in minutes instead of days.

Booking a makeup artist in Malaysia today usually means scrolling Instagram, DM-ing multiple artists, waiting for price lists, and chasing confirmations across WhatsApp threads — often with no guarantee of availability or payment security. Leish! replaces that with:

- **Real-time availability** — see which artists and studios are actually free on your date, not just their portfolio
- **Instant confirmation & secure payment** — Billplz-powered deposits and payments (FPX, cards), no cash-handling guesswork
- **Vetted, portfolio-first profiles** — verified artists with reviews, galleries and transparent pricing
- **Automated reminders & support** — booking confirmations and reminders via email and WhatsApp, so nothing slips through the cracks
- **Rewards & Leish+** — a loyalty program for repeat clients, with a Leish+ subscription plan (from RM29/month) for beauty regulars

Already in early use since mid-2026, Leish! has onboarded early clients and artists across KL & Selangor, with 8 beauty categories live and hundreds of bookings processed through its platform ahead of the public launch.

**Launch offer:** to mark the launch, new clients get **RM30 off their first booking** with code **LEISH30** (valid until 3 November 2026, first 300 redemptions, T&Cs apply).

On the supply side, Leish! is recruiting makeup artists and studios across Malaysia's capital region. **Artists who join during the launch window get a free 3-month Pro listing** (featured placement) and **0% commission on their first 10 bookings**, plus a shareable profile link that turns every artist into their own booking channel.
