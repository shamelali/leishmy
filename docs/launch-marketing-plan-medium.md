## 1. Executive Summary

Same product, same launch date, same campaign — **execution scaled to RM 40,000 / 90 days** with a lean team (founder + one part-time marketer).

1. **Launch Oct 5, 2026** (wedding season) as a *co-hosted pop-up + micro-KOL* launch instead of a full event — partner studios share venue cost and bring their own clients.
2. **Pre-launch Aug 31 – Oct 4 (5 weeks):** 1,000 waitlist + 25 anchor artists (concierge). Artist gate before Day 0.
3. **Paid media concentrated in Meta** (RM 12k) + narrow Google (RM 4k, brand + top-10 keywords). No TikTok ads.
4. **One KOL wave** (6–8 micro creators, 10k–50k followers) with commission-heavy deals to stretch budget.

**North-star metric:** completed bookings per week.
**Day-90 targets:** 800 clients · 40–45 active artists · 15–20 bookings/wk · RM 4.5–6k GMV/wk · CAC ≤ RM 25 · ROAS ≥ 2.5x.

## 6. Channel Plan

### Owned (foundation)
- **Instagram:** 3–4 posts + 7 stories + 1 reel / wk
- **TikTok:** 2 reels / wk (repurpose IG reels)
- **Facebook + bridal/beauty groups:** 3 posts/wk + daily participation
- **Email (Brevo):** waitlist→launch sequence + 1 newsletter/mo
- **WhatsApp API:** confirmations, reminders, monthly promo broadcast
- **SEO:** 1 blog/mo (bridal cost guide, hijab looks) + category pages on /inspiration + /events
- **Artist share links:** always-on growth loop

### Paid (RM 16,000)
| Channel | Budget | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Meta | RM 12,000 | Bridal/beauty interest stacks, KL-Selangor, 20–40; 20% retargeting |
| Google | RM 4,000 | Brand + top-10 keywords ("bridal makeup KL", "MUA near me", "event makeup") |
| TikTok Ads | — | None (out of scope for this tier) |

### Earned & community (RM 19,000)
- **KOLs:** 6–8 micro (10k–50k) — 50% flat fee / 50% booking-code commission (10% of GMV) to share risk
- **Events:** 1 co-hosted pop-up (RM 3k) or 1 wedding expo (RM 6k) — pick by lead-cost comparison
- **Campus:** 2 pop-ups in Cyberjaya (MMU, Limkokwing) with RM30 code
- **PR:** direct pitch to SAYS, The Star, Malay Mail, Selangor Journal, wedding blogs (no agency fee)
- **Community:** giveaways in bridal FB groups (RM500 prize pool)

### Artist acquisition (RM 3,000)
- Concierge onboarding (marketer 0.5 FTE, 15 setups/wk)
- Meta ads targeting working MUAs (RM 1k) — "Switch from DM chaos"
- Artist referral: RM100 + featured slot on referee's 5th booking

## 11. Upgrade / Downgrade Triggers

- **Upgrade to High (RM 96k):** Day-60 checkpoint shows ≥12 bookings/wk, CAC ≤ RM 25, artist retention ≥ 80% → scale paid + wave-2 KOLs for CNY (Jan–Feb 2027) season.
