# Leish! Launch Marketing Plan — Executive Summary
**2026-08-04 · Full plan: `docs/launch-marketing-plan.md`**

## The play
Launch **October 5, 2026** (peak Malaysian wedding season) as Malaysia's first real-time beauty booking platform — kill the "DM 15 MUAs, wait 3 days for a quote" pain. Build supply (artists/studios) first, then drive demand.

## Positioning
- **Clients:** "Book Malaysia's top makeup artists & studios in minutes — real-time availability, instant confirmation, secure payment."
- **Artists:** "Get booked. Get paid. Zero admin."
- **Campaign:** "Never DM for a date again." (clients) · "Get booked. Get paid." (artists)
- **Hero categories:** Bridal · Hijab · Event (8 total: + Editorial, Airbrush, SFX, Hair, Lash)

## Timeline
| Phase | When | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| 0. Pre-launch | Aug 10 – Oct 4 | 2,000 waitlist · 30 bookable artists |
| 1. Launch week | Oct 5–11 | 300 sign-ups · PR + 8–12 KOLs · first bookings |
| 2. Wedding season | Oct 12 – Dec 31 | 25–30 bookings/wk · 60 artists · ROAS 2.5x+ |
| 3. Review | Jan 2027 | Score vs targets · Q1'27 plan · Leish+ public |

## Day-90 targets
1,500 clients · 60+ active artists · 25–30 bookings/wk · RM 7–9k GMV/wk · CAC ≤ RM20 · email 6,000 · social 10,000

## Budget — RM 96,000 / 90 days
- Meta ads RM 29,000 (30%) · Google RM 12,000 (13%) · TikTok RM 6,000 (6%)
- KOLs (2 waves, 16–18 creators) RM 24,000 (25%)
- Events (launch pop-up + expos) RM 12,000 (13%) · PR RM 3,000 (3%)
- Artist acquisition RM 6,000 (6%) · Community/campus RM 4,000 (4%)

## Core offers
- Clients: `LEISH30` — RM30 off first booking (cap 300, Day 30) · Refer-a-friend RM20/RM20
- Artists: free 3-month Pro listing + 0% commission on first 10 bookings · RM100 referral fee + featured slot

## Top 5 risks
1. Too few artists at launch → concierge recruitment from T-6; 30-artist gate before Day 0
2. Demand lags supply → weekly zero-booking check + demand ads
3. Checkout friction (Billplz) → monitor funnel, A/B deposit vs full payment
4. KOL underperformance → 60/40 flat + commission; test wave 1 before wave 2
5. Promo abuse → one code per account, 300 cap
