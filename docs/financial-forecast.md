# Leish.my — Detailed Financial Forecast

**Prepared:** August 2026
**Period:** FY 2026–2029 (36 months)
**Currency:** Malaysian Ringgit (RM)
**Status:** Seed-stage beauty booking marketplace

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Leish.my is a beauty booking marketplace connecting clients with professional makeup artists and studios in Kuala Lumpur & Selangor, Malaysia. The platform operates on a commission-based marketplace model, taking a 20% cut on each booking.

**Current Traction (as of Aug 2026):**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Registered Clients | 88+ |
| Total Bookings | 104+ |
| Pro Artists | 4+ |
| Beauty Categories | 8 |
| Service Area | KL & Selangor |
| Avg Booking Value | RM 250–350 |
| Platform Commission | 20% |

**Financial Highlights:**

- **Year 1 Revenue (FY2026):** RM 180,000 – 250,000
- **Year 2 Revenue (FY2027):** RM 720,000 – 960,000
- **Year 3 Revenue (FY2028):** RM 2,160,000 – 2,880,000
- **Break-even:** Month 18–20 (base case)
- **Seed Funding Required:** RM 1,200,000 (18 months runway)

### C. Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| GMV | Gross Merchandise Value — total booking value before commission |
| LTV | Customer Lifetime Value — total revenue from a user over their lifetime |
| CAC | Customer Acquisition Cost — total marketing + sales cost to acquire one user |
