## 3. When to choose which tier

| Choose | If… |
|---|---|
| **HIGH (RM 96k)** | You want maximum market capture in the Oct–Dec wedding season; team exists; seed runway supports it (per financial forecast); comfortable scaling/optimizing paid weekly. |
| **MEDIUM (RM 40k)** | You want a real launch with paid + KOLs but lean team; you need a clear ROAS gate before committing more; likely the best default for a seed-stage startup. |
| **LOW (RM 10k)** | You must prove demand first; founder-led; willing to accept a slow ramp in exchange for cheap learning; plan to upgrade for Q1'27 if targets hit. |

---

## 4. Upgrade / downgrade paths

- **Low → Medium:** Day-60 checkpoint — ≥8 bookings/wk, CAC ≤ RM 15, artist retention ≥ 80% → move for Q1'27 (CNY season).
- **Medium → High:** Day-60 checkpoint — ≥12 bookings/wk, CAC ≤ RM 25 → scale paid + wave-2 KOLs for CNY.
- **High → Medium:** paid CAC > RM 35 by Day 30 → cut Google + half of Meta; re-plan at Day 60.
- **Medium → Low:** paid CAC > RM 35 by Day 30 → shift to community/artist-network channels.

---

## 5. What never changes across tiers

- Launch date logic (Oct 5 = peak wedding season) — tier only scales intensity
- Supply-first: artists recruited and gated before demand campaigns
- North-star metric: completed bookings per week
- Offers anchored on first-booking discount (LEISH30) + artist free-Pro
- Weekly KPI dashboard and ROAS gates (tighter as budget grows)
- Product levers: artist share links, referral, WhatsApp flow, Brevo, GA4/Meta events
