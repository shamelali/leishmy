## 1. Executive Summary

**Founder-led quiet launch** — near-zero paid media, growth built on community, the artist network, referrals, and barter. The goal is not scale; it's **proof**: hit consistent weekly bookings at CAC ≤ RM 15 and unlock a bigger budget for Q1'27 (CNY season).

1. **Soft launch Oct 5, 2026** — no event, no paid splash: waitlist email + organic posts + `LEISH30`.
2. **Pre-launch Sep 7 – Oct 4 (4 weeks):** 400 waitlist + 20 anchor artists (concierge, founder-led). Artist gate before Day 0.
3. **Growth loops instead of ads:** artist share links (built into the product), referral from Day 1, bridal FB group seeding (free), nano-KOL barter.
4. **Spend discipline:** RM 2.5k Meta retargeting only; pause anything <1.5x ROAS after 2 weeks.

**North-star metric:** completed bookings per week.
**Day-90 targets:** 300 clients · 25 active artists · 6–10 bookings/wk · RM 1.8–3k GMV/wk · CAC ≤ RM 15 · email 1,200.

## 6. Channel Plan

### Owned first (≈ zero cost — 85% of the work)
- **Instagram:** 3 posts + daily stories + 1 reel / wk (batch on 2 content days)
- **TikTok:** 1–2 reels / wk (repurpose; founder speaks directly to camera)
- **Facebook + bridal/beauty groups:** daily participation, 3 value posts/wk (guides, artist spotlights, offer drops)
- **Email (Brevo):** waitlist→launch sequence + 1 newsletter/mo
- **WhatsApp API:** confirmations, reminders, opt-in broadcast monthly
- **SEO:** 1 blog/mo, long-tail only ("hijab makeup KL", "bridal MUA Selangor")
- **Artist share links:** the #1 acquisition loop — ask every artist to share their link weekly
- **Founder LinkedIn:** weekly posts on "building a beauty marketplace in Malaysia"

### Paid (RM 2,500 — retargeting only)
- Meta retargeting from week 2: site visitors + waitlist abandoners, RM30 creative
- **No Google ads, no TikTok ads, no prospecting** at this tier

### Earned & community (RM 5,500)
- **Nano KOLs (2k–20k):** barter (free service) + 10–15% of GMV via booking codes — zero/low flat fees
- **Ambassador program:** top 10 clients get RM50 credit/mo for 2 posts — cheap UGC engine
- **Events:** 1 co-hosted pop-up (partner studio hosts venue; we run booking + offer) + 1 campus pop-up
- **PR:** direct emails to wedding bloggers + founder's network; no agency, no paid distribution
- **Community:** giveaways in bridal FB groups (RM 500), UGC contest (RM 50 × 5 vouchers)

### Artist acquisition (RM 1,000)
- Concierge recruitment is founder-led and free; budget is referral fees only (RM100 × 10)
- Offer **free 6-month Pro listing** (vs 3 months in other tiers) to compensate for lower traffic

## 11. Upgrade Triggers

- **Upgrade to Medium (RM 40k):** Day-60 checkpoint — ≥8 bookings/wk, CAC ≤ RM 15, artist retention ≥ 80% → move for Q1'27 (CNY wedding/event season, Jan–Feb). This is the primary goal of the Low tier.
- **Upgrade to High (RM 96k):** only after Medium tier proves ≥12 bookings/wk at CAC ≤ RM 25.
- **No upgrade:** if Day-90 GMV/wk < RM 1,500, diagnose (supply? checkout? demand?) before spending more — the Low tier is the cheapest place to learn this.
